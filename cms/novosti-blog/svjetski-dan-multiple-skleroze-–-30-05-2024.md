U večernjim satima 29.05.2024. povodom Svjetskog dana multiple skleroze narančastim bojama zasvijetlilo je [Gradsko kazalište "Zorin dom" Karlovac](https://www.facebook.com/zorin.karlovac?__cft__%5b0%5d=AZUFeSLt-peAVTH-58rhlJb2TVdvB5hOzLfk2BE_dlhWS6EE0-K-1pegTeFw0vK4j_xMNtR3Tt6bPE5bgMBXncE3iZi1Pn-JLXeiJnZo-T6DqVddWEsKlepZO0Wa2nPO_SDCjC6gX2be7ozL5V39k6dt_dwgClyxCxMjXjQ7EAJL9t7tgCa4aNE_KmyLobQMhhogwAw0nV9hybowQ-_6VgVP&__tn__=-%5dK-R). O samom Svjetskom danu multiple skleroze izvijestili su i lokalni mediji - [KaPortal](https://kaportal.net.hr/aktualno/vijesti/4450125/i-u-karlovcu-obiljezen-svjetski-dan-multiple-skleroze-tim-povodom-gradsko-kazaliste-zorin-dom-zasjalo-narancastom-bojom/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAAR2nAVBI-1MsFPlK7s4Im0ene_fYqcyZTbvQ25KDsV6HNvXgH0iZmL0US1A_aem_AYVNlZjhRiZ7tngsc7Rr_zEb-h6HgsfDqgiXyWETgmBFdQOcyK6wHPqop5wqRNVATuIkcocJDmPjs1QZ4bmfLLdl) , [Trend mediji](https://trend.com.hr/2024/05/30/kao-podrsku-oboljelima-od-multiple-skleroze-zorin-dom-je-svijetlio-u-narancastoj-boji/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAAR1a6u6pHv_y0MAkmlpmtAJwHTfAXQcGOIFrOAa9NJrwtne4M5kofYYbHOM_aem_AYUIdIf_VSI9LmrXLTlWLCGKTaRTGJjmLz42ToP1Pvznt2oOzvGIGRACXVnY0g5r-BWli2OeKvNXN1p9ORVUFo6p) , [Karlovački](https://karlovacki.hr/svjetski-dan-multiple-skleroze-obiljezen-i-u-karlovcu/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAAR1ussrxpkNx9r8yhdrJYKqN8Zkp3FqoCPdTJqN2pgA9yFs2jPgjWbU2e7I_aem_AYUeKvT_eqzvL87Yw4AdDUnZI8TxsJP2TMcWeABBpJilpSd7I9ht5Y6kfwjtB4wBRiCOp-M_raPIEQxXXbSCxGv-) kojima uz Gradsko kazalište “Zorin dom“ zahvaljujemo na doprinosu osvještavanja javnosti i zajednice o multipli sklerozi.

Svjetski dan multiple skleroze obilježava se 30.5., a krovni [SavezDruštava MultipleSkleroze](https://www.facebook.com/savezdrustavamshrvatske?__cft__%5b0%5d=AZUFeSLt-peAVTH-58rhlJb2TVdvB5hOzLfk2BE_dlhWS6EE0-K-1pegTeFw0vK4j_xMNtR3Tt6bPE5bgMBXncE3iZi1Pn-JLXeiJnZo-T6DqVddWEsKlepZO0Wa2nPO_SDCjC6gX2be7ozL5V39k6dt_dwgClyxCxMjXjQ7EAJL9t7tgCa4aNE_KmyLobQMhhogwAw0nV9hybowQ-_6VgVP&__tn__=-%5dK-R)provodi kampanju [\#jasamviseodmsa](https://www.facebook.com/hashtag/jasamviseodmsa?__eep__=6&__cft__%5b0%5d=AZUFeSLt-peAVTH-58rhlJb2TVdvB5hOzLfk2BE_dlhWS6EE0-K-1pegTeFw0vK4j_xMNtR3Tt6bPE5bgMBXncE3iZi1Pn-JLXeiJnZo-T6DqVddWEsKlepZO0Wa2nPO_SDCjC6gX2be7ozL5V39k6dt_dwgClyxCxMjXjQ7EAJL9t7tgCa4aNE_KmyLobQMhhogwAw0nV9hybowQ-_6VgVP&__tn__=*NK-R) kojoj se i mi pridružujemo. Multipla skleroza je kronična neurološka bolest s kojom se može kvalitetno živjeti uz odgovarajuće liječenje i način života, no za to je potrebno i razumijevanje i podrška okoline, obitelji, partnera, prijatelja, poslodavca...
